# Gas Sponsor asset provenance

The application reuses the repository-owned production media already shipped for this MiniApp:

- `public/gas-sponsor-refill-station.webp` — primary in-app refill-station scene.
- `public/logo.webp` / `public/banner.webp` — catalog identity media.
- `CoinArt` from `apps/shared/art` — canonical GAS token artwork used throughout the interface.

The scene image is used as an in-app environment with a high-contrast foreground control card, not as a promotional cover pasted behind form controls. No emoji, inline SVG illustration, generated placeholder or per-app imitation GAS logo is used.
