# Gomoku Art Assets

These project-specific game assets are original artwork created for the Neo
MiniApps learning project.

- Source: programmatically generated vector art (layered SVG compositions
  rasterized with sharp at 2x and downscaled to webp); no third-party asset
  packs were used
- Generated: 2026-07-25 by `scripts/generate-gomoku-art.mjs`
- License: original to this repository, usable under the same terms as the
  repository itself

Included assets:

- `table-linen.webp` — woven cloth backdrop behind the board
- `board-wood.webp` — lacquered board panel with grain and bevelled frame
- `stone-black.webp` — polished black stone with rim light
- `stone-white.webp` — polished white stone with rim light
- `last-move.webp` — ring marker for the most recent placement
- `win-glow.webp` — stretchable capsule glow traced along the winning five
- `result-seal.webp` — victory seal stamped over the lobby preview
- `badge-easy.webp`
- `badge-medium.webp`
- `badge-hard.webp`
