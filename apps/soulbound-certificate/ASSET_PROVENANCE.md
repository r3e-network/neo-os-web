The active credential experience uses the two real WebP certificate resources. It does not draw the certificate, seal, QR, or achievement art with emoji, ASCII, placeholder boxes, CSS illustration, or handcrafted inline SVG. Interface symbols come from `lucide-react`; the QR is generated from the exact public verification URL by the declared `qrcode` dependency.

## Compatibility files

`public/logo.svg` and `public/banner.svg` are outputs of the repository logo system retained for compatibility. The app entry point and manifest use the WebP variants, so these SVG files are not visible in the shipped Soulbound Certificate experience.

## Production boundary

The current imagery is visually suitable and was inspected locally at source resolution: warm daylight, cream paper, dark green/gold detail, and large blank regions that support high-contrast foreground copy. Public distribution still needs an owner to attach the original generation record or an explicit internal-use/license statement for `certificate-paper.webp`, `certificate-atelier.webp`, and `planned-atlas-05.png`. Until that record exists, these assets should not be represented as externally licensed stock or as official Neo artwork.
