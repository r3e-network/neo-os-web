Sudoku Arena gameplay assets
============================

These sprites were generated specifically for Neo MiniApps in this workspace
using OpenAI image generation, then manually cropped and composed into the
Sudoku Arena play surface.

The set includes board tiles, interaction states, pencil-note props,
difficulty seals, the sealed puzzle envelope, solved badge, and reward trophy.
They are original project assets and are not copied from a third-party game.
