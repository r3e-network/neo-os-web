Date: 2026-07-18. Sources: `audit-findings-2026-07/{miniapps,morpheus,aa}.json` (41 findings), `duplication.json`, `joint-baseline-2026-07-18.md`. All findings verified against actual code with file:line citations during the audit.

## 1. Executive summary

- **0 critical, 6 high, 11 medium, 24 low** across the three repos.
- No solvency, accounting, or signature-verification breaks found: PlatformRegistry fee/liability accounting, RewardGame settle math (`heldForApp == pool + Σcredits`), AppAccount gating, AA plugin routing invariant, and the private-kernel fulfillment digest (byte-exact vs the real kernel, independently recomputed) all verified sound.
- The dominant theme is **inconsistent administrative safety**: several fund-holding contracts allow *instant* admin upgrades/rotations while their siblings enforce 7-day/24h timelocks. Second theme: **cross-repo drift** (oracle hash, envelope copies) caused by generated-artifact coupling without a single resolution flow.
